# Plaintiff's Verified Bill of Particulars

**NYSCEF Doc. No. 5** · Served 2026-04-30 · Harrison & Volt (Plaintiff)

> Sample / fictional document for the demo matter. Not legal advice.

In response to defendant's demand, plaintiff alleges (summary):

- **Date/time of occurrence:** January 9, 2025, at approximately 8:40 a.m.
- **Location:** Interior lobby of 480 Lexington Avenue, approximately 10 feet inside the
  Lexington Avenue entrance, near the elevator bank.
- **Condition:** Accumulation of water, slush, and tracked-in snow on the polished stone
  floor; no mats or warning cones present.
- **Notice:** Plaintiff alleges **constructive notice**, contending the condition existed for
  a sufficient length of time (alleged 45+ minutes) for defendant's employees to discover and
  remedy it; plaintiff reserves the right to allege actual notice following discovery.
- **Statutes alleged:** Common-law negligence; NYC Administrative Code provisions regarding
  safe maintenance of premises (to be specified).

## Injuries Alleged

- Displaced fracture, right distal radius, requiring **ORIF surgery** (1/14/2025);
- Post-surgical hardware; reduced range of motion and grip strength;
- Aggravation of pre-existing conditions (denied/unspecified);
- Alleged permanency and need for future hardware removal and physical therapy.

## Defense Notes (work product)

- Specials are **unsupported** pending production of medical bills, paid/charged amounts
  (CPLR 4545 collateral source), and earnings documentation. Lost earnings as a "freelance
  designer" require tax returns/1099s — demand in combined discovery.
- Constructive-notice theory hinges on the alleged 45-minute duration — **no evidentiary
  basis identified yet**; test at EBT and via maintenance logs.
