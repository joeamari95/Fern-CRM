# Defendant's Demand for a Verified Bill of Particulars

**DRAFT — to be served by 2026-06-05** (CPLR 3042)
Defendant Acme Property Holdings LLC → Plaintiff

> Sample / fictional document for the demo matter. Not legal advice.

Defendant demands that plaintiff serve a verified bill of particulars setting forth:

1. The **specific date and time** of the alleged occurrence.
2. The **exact location** within the lobby where plaintiff allegedly fell, with reference to
   fixed objects (entrance doors, elevator bank, etc.).
3. A **description of the alleged dangerous condition** (substance, size, color, depth).
4. The basis for the claim of **actual notice** — state when, how, and to whom notice was
   given, including names of any defendant employees.
5. The basis for the claim of **constructive notice** — state how long the condition is
   alleged to have existed prior to the occurrence.
6. Each **statute, ordinance, rule, or regulation** alleged to have been violated.
7. A full **statement of injuries**, specifying which are claimed to be permanent.
8. **Special damages**: itemized medical expenses to date, projected future expenses, and
   lost earnings (with employer/income basis).
9. Periods of **confinement** to bed, home, and hospital.
