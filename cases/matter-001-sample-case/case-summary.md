## Facts

Plaintiff Margaret Doe, age 58, alleges that on the morning of January 9, 2025 she
slipped and fell on a wet interior lobby floor at **480 Lexington Avenue, New York, NY**,
a commercial office building owned by our client, Acme Property Holdings LLC. Plaintiff
alleges the floor was wet from tracked-in snow/slush and that no warning cones or mats were
present. She alleges a **displaced fracture of the right distal radius** requiring open
reduction and internal fixation (ORIF) surgery on January 14, 2025.

## Liability Posture

The central liability questions are:

1. **Notice** — whether Acme had actual or constructive notice of the wet condition. The
   condition is alleged to be transient (tracked-in precipitation), which supports a
   *no constructive notice* defense absent evidence the condition existed for an
   appreciable time.
2. **Delegation** — snow/ice removal and lobby maintenance were contracted to the managing
   agent, **Beacon Facility Services Inc.**, under a written service agreement. We have
   asserted a cross-claim against Beacon and tendered defense/indemnity (see
   `emails/2026-05-19-beacon-tender.md`).
3. **Storm in progress** — weather records for 1/9/2025 are being pulled to assess a
   *storm-in-progress* defense, which would suspend the duty to remediate during an
   ongoing storm.

## Damages

Plaintiff's Bill of Particulars alleges **$185,000 in special damages** (past medical,
projected future treatment, and lost earnings as a freelance graphic designer). A defense
orthopedic IME (Dr. Helen Brandt) is to be scheduled after the records exchange.
