## Bottom line

Defensible case on **notice** and **delegation**. Early facts (7:30 a.m. mopping + cone
placement, transient tracked-in precipitation, no identified evidence of the 45-minute
duration plaintiff alleges) favor a no-constructive-notice posture. Primary exposure drivers
are (a) the cone's status at the time of the fall and (b) the unconfirmed CCTV / possible
spoliation issue.

  risk. High priority.
- **Cone timing** — security witness Okafor is pivotal; lock down at EBT.
- **Damages** — ORIF surgery makes this a real-injury case; even with strong liability,
