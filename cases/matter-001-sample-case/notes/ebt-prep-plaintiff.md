# EBT Prep — Plaintiff Margaret Doe

> **ATTORNEY WORK PRODUCT — PRIVILEGED.** Sample / fictional content for the demo matter.

**Deposition:** Plaintiff Margaret Doe · Target date 2026-06-18 · Examining: Fern (Defense)

## Objectives

1. Pin down the **exact location, time, and mechanism** of the fall.
2. Lock plaintiff into the **constructive-notice timeline** (the alleged 45 minutes) and
   expose the lack of factual basis.
3. Establish whether plaintiff **saw the warning cone** / was looking where she was walking.
4. Develop **comparative-fault** facts (footwear, phone, hurry, familiarity with lobby).
5. Probe **pre-existing conditions** and prior wrist/orthopedic history (causation).
6. Develop **damages** facts: actual out-of-pocket vs. insurance-paid; freelance income basis.

## Key topic outline

- Background; familiarity with the building; route taken that morning.
- Weather that morning; what she observed entering the lobby (snow/slush on her own shoes?).
- The condition: describe it — size, color, depth; did she see it before/after?
- The cone/mats: did she see any warning signs or cones? Where?
- The fall mechanics; what part of her body hit; who responded (security/Okafor).
- Post-incident: reports made, statements given, photos taken by her or companions.
- Treatment timeline; prior injuries; work history and income documentation.

## Documents to have ready

- Incident report (security); maintenance/cone log for 1/9 (from client production).
- Bill of Particulars (for impeachment on the 45-minute claim).
- Any plaintiff social media / photos (if obtained).
- Certified weather records (when received).
