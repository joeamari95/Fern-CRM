## Verified Complaint (summary)

1. **Parties** — Plaintiff Margaret Doe is a resident of New York County. Defendant Acme
   Property Holdings LLC is a domestic limited liability company that owns and operates the
   premises at 480 Lexington Avenue, New York, NY.

2. **The Occurrence** — On or about January 9, 2025, plaintiff lawfully entered the lobby of
   the premises when she was caused to slip and fall on a wet, slippery, and dangerous
   floor condition.

3. **Negligence** — Defendant, its agents, servants and/or employees were negligent in the
   ownership, operation, management, maintenance, and control of the premises; in allowing a
   dangerous wet condition to exist; in failing to remedy same; in failing to warn; and in
   failing to provide mats or warning signs, although defendant had actual and/or
   constructive notice of the condition.

4. **Injuries** — As a result, plaintiff sustained serious and permanent personal injuries,
   including a fracture of the right wrist requiring surgery, and has incurred medical
   expenses and lost earnings.

**WHEREFORE**, plaintiff demands judgment against defendant in an amount exceeding the
jurisdictional limits of all lower courts, together with the costs and disbursements of
this action.
