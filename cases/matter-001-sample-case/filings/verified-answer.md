# Verified Answer with Affirmative Defenses and Cross-Claim

**NYSCEF Doc. No. 2** · Filed 2025-03-20 · Wilson Elser (for Defendant Acme)

> Sample / fictional document for the demo matter. Not legal advice.

Defendant **ACME PROPERTY HOLDINGS LLC**, by its attorneys Wilson Elser Moskowitz Edelman &
Dicker LLP, as and for its Verified Answer, responds as follows:

## Responses

1. **Denies** knowledge or information sufficient to form a belief as to the truth of the
   allegations regarding plaintiff's residency and injuries.
2. **Denies** each and every allegation of negligence, notice, and dangerous condition.
3. **Admits** only that it owns the premises at 480 Lexington Avenue, New York, NY.

## Affirmative Defenses

1. **Comparative negligence** — Plaintiff's own culpable conduct caused or contributed to
   the occurrence (CPLR Art. 14-A).
2. **No notice** — Defendant had neither actual nor constructive notice of the alleged
   condition.
3. **Transient condition** — Any condition was transitory and not present for a sufficient
   time to permit remediation.
4. **Storm in progress** — Any precipitation-related condition arose during an ongoing storm,
   suspending any duty to remediate.
5. **Delegation** — Maintenance and snow/ice removal were delegated by written contract to a
   third party (Beacon Facility Services Inc.).
6. **Failure to mitigate** damages.
7. **Collateral source** (CPLR 4545).
8. **Limitation of liability** under applicable law.
9. **Assumption of risk.**

## Cross-Claim Against Beacon Facility Services Inc.

Upon information and belief, any liability being denied, plaintiff's damages were caused by
the negligence of co-defendant/third party **Beacon Facility Services Inc.**, which
contracted to perform snow/ice removal and lobby maintenance at the premises. Defendant Acme
is entitled to **common-law and contractual indemnification and/or contribution** from
Beacon.

**WHEREFORE**, defendant demands judgment dismissing the complaint, together with
indemnification/contribution from Beacon, and the costs of this action.
